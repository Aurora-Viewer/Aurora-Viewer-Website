Performance

Built for every core of your machine.

Aurora shares the work across your processor and hands the heavy lifting to your graphics card. While one frame is drawn, the next one is already being prepared.

Arrive without a freeze

Textures and meshes decode in the background, straight into GPU memory. What you look at downloads first.

Places you know, at once

Textures are cached on disk ready for the graphics card, in about half the video memory.

It holds on

A network blip no longer logs you out: Aurora reconnects you right where you were.

Made for today's hardware

Vulkan, Metal on macOS, the dedicated graphics card on a laptop, and AMD FSR 3.1 upscaling on every brand of card.

Features

Everything you know, rebuilt for today.

Fast, and smooth

A viewer that keeps up with you.

  • Instant teleports within a region; the others show real progress
  • Fewer hitches in a crowd, smoother avatars around you
  • A full video memory no longer freezes the viewer, kept within your card's budget
  • Your whole inventory loads in the background, right from login
  • Avatars out of the cloud about three times sooner, your whole outfit worn at login

A sharper, richer picture

Modern light, without changing the look you know.

  • Ambient occlusion (GTAO) and sun contact shadows
  • Clean shadows and highlights that hold still as you move
  • Graphics levels matched to your machine at first launch
  • PBR terrain, and the Legacy Viewer's HDR switch for those who prefer the older look

The Second Life you know

Same world, same rules, nothing to relearn.

  • PBR and legacy materials, EEP skies, mirrors, water and glow
  • Voice with noise suppression, IMs, groups and a searchable history
  • Shape and clothing editors, gestures, a built-in AO and your own sky and water
  • RLVa, opt-in, for the experiences and outfits that rely on it
  • Portals you open and walk through, shared with your friends
  • Mouselook with a free cursor on Alt, and the crosshair you choose
  • OpenSim grids and the Hypergrid, with a grid manager and an avatar baked where the grid does not bake

An interface made for today

Flat, calm and readable, arranged your way.

  • In English, French and now Japanese (日本語), following your system language
  • Glass island bars and a notification center with history
  • One search for residents, places, events, groups and classifieds
  • An inventory that widens into an explorer, and a second one beside it
  • Immersive mode: the whole interface hidden but the chat
  • Your outfits sorted in folders, right from Appearance
  • The Legacy Viewer’s toolbar buttons, all there: Quick preferences, a web browser, the voice controls
  • Your friends' name tags in their colour, and an L$ balance you can hide while you stream

Conversations your way

Chat that shows what matters, and stays out of the way.

  • Pop a conversation out, or drag it into its own window
  • Previews for pictures, Bluesky and X posts, and videos that play right in the conversation
  • A GIF picker with your own free GIPHY or KLIPY key
  • A spell checker, @mentions, and gestures completed as you type
  • A thumbnail per conversation beside the chat bar, bubbles above heads if you like
  • Arabic and Hebrew read right to left, and L$ payments show as a card
  • Multi-line messages, your script errors in nearby chat, and typing dots on pictures

Made for every eye

Settings that help, gathered on one page.

  • An Accessibility page, one click away from the login screen
  • A high-contrast palette and the OpenDyslexic font
  • Your own accent, background and text colours, kept readable
  • Volume sliders that follow your ear
  • Text up to twice its size

Updates in one click

On Windows, at launch, Aurora offers the new version, downloads it in the background, installs it and restarts your session. Before you agree, it tells you whether your cache and settings are kept.

  • Downloaded in the background
  • Checked against its published hash
  • Rolled back if anything goes wrong

Photo mode

A camera in your hands.

The photo mode opens full screen under a real viewfinder: autofocus that finds faces, lens rings for focal length, aperture and exposure, and a live histogram. Frame, focus, shoot.

Autofocus that finds faces

AF-S, AF-C or manual. On an avatar, the focus locks between the eyes and follows them.

Lens rings

Focal length, aperture and exposure on rings you drag, or on the mouse wheel.

Real depth of field

On by default, with focus peaking in manual focus to see exactly what is sharp.

Light under control

A live histogram, exposure lock, and a level to keep the horizon straight, or tilt it.

Up to 8K

4K by default, in PNG, JPEG or BMP, to your disk, your inventory or your profile feed.

Free camera, self-timer

Orbit, free or first person, and a 2 or 10 second timer to get into your pose.

Isolate your subject

Your avatar alone, or the avatars and objects you click, on a transparent background: a PNG ready to use, soft hair included.

66 filters and effects

The Legacy Viewer's 30 filters and 36 of Aurora's own, from film grain to VHS. Stack up to six, each with its strength and its own sliders, and compare before and after.

Effects that see depth

A haze that grows with distance, a true 3D miniature, a blurred background, the subject standing out. And your own .cube LUTs from Lightroom or Resolve.

Freeze the world

Every avatar, animation and moving thing holds still while your camera stays free, to line up the perfect angle on a pose.

Camera presets

Save a whole shot by name and bring it back in one click: where the camera stands, the lens, the filters and the light.

Quiet snapshots

The shutter is heard around you and your avatar strikes the snapshot pose, as in the Legacy Viewer. One switch keeps both quiet.

Night sky

The real stars, right above you.

The 8,920 stars you can see with the naked eye, in their true places and colours, and the Milky Way. The sky turns with the region’s day and follows today’s season. Choose where on Earth you look up from, from Paris to Sydney, and how bright the stars shine.

Preferences › Graphics › Sky and environment, also in Personal Lighting. The Legacy Viewer’s random stars stay one click away.

Creator tools

Make it, see it, before you upload it.

Your Blender mesh shows in the viewer while you edit it, a poser sets up your photos joint by joint, and the build tools stay out of your way.

LiveMesh

Edit in Blender and see it at once, with nothing uploaded. Rigged meshes follow your own avatar. The plug-in ships with the viewer and installs itself, with an SL / Bento starter rig and an import with its LODs prepared.

Objects and linksets

Static meshes become local objects: place them on the ground, move, rotate, resize, attach them to your body or HUD, and detach them.

A poser for photos

Pose yourself, your animesh or nearby avatars who agree to it, joint by joint. Save poses to a library, export BVH, or import them as an animation.

IK workbench

Reusable pose projects that keep chosen bones on their targets, with contacts that compatible viewers apply to nearby avatars.

Build tools

A compact editor you arrange yourself, larger material previews, a live preview of the prim you create, and outlines that stay while you move. Snap to objects, or place on any surface with a hologram of where it lands.

Area search

Find the objects rezzed around you as you type: name, owner, creator, group or UUID, in the parcel or the regions next door. Filter, sort, then inspect, zoom to or copy.

Particle editor

Shape a particle effect on your prim and watch it live, then put the matching script into it. No llParticleSystem written by hand.

Animation explorer

See which object or resident plays an animation on you, preview it on a stand-in, then stop it, revoke the object's permissions or block it.

Menus your way

Arrange the right-click menus of objects, land, avatars, attachments and HUDs, and edit an object's properties from its profile.

Your way

New interface, or the one you know. Window by window.

Prefer the layout you know? Every redesigned window keeps its Legacy version one click away.

Preferences › Aurora: one choice per window, or all at once.

All release notes
November 11, 2026

Aurora 0.9.6: outfit showcase, auto portraits and 65 photo looks

Appearance's Outfit gallery becomes a showcase: large pictures of your outfits, a rail for favourites, outfits without a picture and folders, and wear in one click (the small tiles stay in Preferences › Aurora). Auto portrait frames you in a studio for the outfit's free picture, Take the portraits does every outfit in one go, and an optional HD picture costs the grid's upload price. The photo LUT tab now holds 20 looks by Czar Fenris and 45 by Nanner Pudding, thank you both! Your cache and settings are kept.

→
November 11, 2026

The Milky Way from real stars, and Rendering types

The Milky Way is now drawn from the 2.5 million stars of the Tycho-2 Catalogue, with its star clouds and dark lanes, and Star hour can show the sky by the real clock. Advanced › Rendering types hides terrain, water, objects, avatars or particles to see behind them, like the Legacy Viewer. A Mac starts at the display's logical resolution with Mid-High quality, AMD RX 9000 cards keep their contact shadows, and HUD media answer first in busy regions.

November 11, 2026

Poser, building, inventory and fixes

Hold Space to brake your avatar, like the Legacy Viewer. The poser gets Save as… and shows where a pose went. Move handles are easier to grab, land selections snap to the parcel grid, and glTF materials keep 2K maps. Favorites lists starred items without their folders, and the delete confirmation can be turned off. Region / Estate asks before a change that can send people home. Thanks to anne-skydancer, circuskid, emmelinedevere, Gaalrauch, KanikaBreed, LisaScheers, Marakiinfinity, Memekiller, Nowalski and Odessa-Draekavik.
